VocaLingo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VocaLingo handles information when you use the VocaLingo English language learning application.

1. Information We Collect

VocaLingo is designed primarily as an English language learning application. The application may process information required to provide its learning features, including speech input used for speaking practice and application usage information stored locally on the device.

VocaLingo does not require users to create an account in order to use the core learning features.

2. Microphone and Speech Recognition

VocaLingo requests microphone access when you use speaking and pronunciation practice features.

Speech recognition is used to process the words you speak and to compare your spoken response with the expected English dialogue or exercise content.

Microphone access is only used when you activate a speech-related feature. You can control microphone permission through your device settings.

3. Text-to-Speech

VocaLingo uses text-to-speech technology to provide spoken English dialogue and reading practice.

Text-to-speech processing may be provided by the Android system or an installed speech service on your device.

4. Dictionary and Vocabulary Features

VocaLingo provides dictionary and word-meaning features to support English learning.

Dictionary information used by the application may be stored as part of the application's local data. These features are intended to help users understand unfamiliar English words while studying.

5. WebView Content

Some VocaLingo learning content is displayed inside the application using an embedded WebView.

The WebView is used to display and operate the application's English learning dialogues, exercises, tests, and related educational content.

6. Data Storage

VocaLingo stores certain application data locally on the user's device, including learning progress and application preferences required for the operation of the application.

The amount and type of locally stored information may change as the application is updated and new learning features are introduced.

7. Advertising

VocaLingo currently does not display advertisements.

8. Third-Party Services

VocaLingo may use Android system services and third-party technology libraries required for features such as speech recognition, text-to-speech, and application functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. VocaLingo does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party service providers.

9. Children's Privacy

VocaLingo is an educational application intended for general English language learning.

VocaLingo does not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or accounts.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You can manage application permissions, including microphone access, through your Android device settings.

You may also remove locally stored application data or uninstall the application using the standard Android settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
